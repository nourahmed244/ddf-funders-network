About this data

Each grant program here has the Doris Duke Foundation (DDF) and at least one other funder behind it, found on dorisduke.org and in partners' announcements. Categories use DDF's official program names (Arts, Building Bridges, Child Well-being, Environment, Medical Research). "Across programs" is not a DDF program: it marks foundation-wide funds such as Humanity AI and the NYC COVID-19 Response & Impact Fund.

Edge width is the total size of the programs two funders share, not what either gave; DDF's own share is rarely public. Dashed edges mean the program size was not disclosed. Node size is total assets from each funder's latest IRS 990-PF or audited statement (mostly 2024); filings mix book and market value, so treat sizes as approximate. Faint nodes: no figure found, or the funder is not an endowed foundation (for example the NEA or the American Heart Association). Coordinated pledges where money was not pooled, such as the 2020 five-foundation payout increase, are left out.
